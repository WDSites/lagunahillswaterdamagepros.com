Laguna Woods Village: 12,736 homes built between 1963 and 1985

In 1962 Ross Cortese bought part of the old Moulton Ranch to build a community for residents 52 and older, and the first gates opened on September 10, 1964. The city itself incorporated on March 24, 1999, but the neighborhood that shaped it is the Village. Today it has 12,736 dwelling units on 2,095 acres, built in phases from 1963 to 1985.

Three mutuals share that housing. United Laguna Woods Mutual is a stock cooperative with 6,323 homes, Third Laguna Hills Mutual is a condominium with 6,102, and Laguna Woods Mutual No. Fifty has 311 homes in two high-rises. Which one you belong to decides who repairs what, so keep your governing documents within reach.

The Village counts 2,584 residential buildings, 509 laundry facilities and 82 elevators, and at least one occupant of each home must be 55 or older. A laundry room or elevator pit is shared plumbing and shared floor, so a leak there is a building matter, not just yours.

Shared walls and old buildings: what to check

Water in a multi-unit building moves through floor and ceiling cavities, along pipe chases and between wall studs. A stain on your bedroom ceiling may start two units away. Crews use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to follow the wet path before any drywall is opened.

A general consideration: buildings put up between 1963 and 1985 have had decades of service, so supply lines, shutoff valves and water heater connections deserve a periodic look. That is not a claim about any particular pipe material. A plumber can tell you what is behind your wall.
