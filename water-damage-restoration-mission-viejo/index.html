The water problems that come with Mission Viejo's hills

Three dams sit inside Mission Viejo: Upper Oso, El Toro Reservoir and the earth-fill dam across the Oso Creek canyon that holds Lake Mission Viejo. The city's 2024 draft Local Hazard Mitigation Plan treats dam inundation as a hazard. Ask the city how it is mapped for your address, since homeowners policies generally exclude flood.

The same plan calls flood zones a recurring concern because heavy rain meets hilly terrain, and it says the eastern parts of the city carry varying degrees of landslide potential. For a homeowner, that means water often arrives from outside, across a patio or under a garage door, instead of from a pipe. Outside water carries soil, so crews treat it as Category 3, based on IICRC S500 categories.

Two storm periods are on the record. The December 2010 storm (FEMA disaster DR-1952) eroded the Aliso Creek Bike Trail Bridge and the Jeronimo Creek drainage near Arbolitos and Silleros, and federal emergency declarations in 2023 for storms, flooding and mudslides included the city. Walk your downspouts, swales and slope drains before winter and photograph them, so a later claim has a baseline.

If water is still spreading: before we arrive

Find the source

Close the angle stop under the fixture, or the house main if the pipe is inside a wall. For slope or street water, the goal is to steer it away from the door.

Check the panel first

Breakers for wet areas can go off only when the panel itself is dry and you reach it on dry floor. If not, call 911 or the utility.

Lift what you can

Move rugs, laptops and paperwork to dry shelves. Leave heavy furniture where it is.

Stay out of runoff

Keep children and pets away from mud or water that came from outside. It may carry soil and sewage.

Photograph, then call

Which services Mission Viejo calls usually need, in order

Storm and slope calls usually begin with flood damage restoration. Muddy water means carpet, pad and low drywall are judged for removal, and surfaces are cleaned before drying starts. Then water mitigation takes over: moisture meters map the wet area, and air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry the rest.

A burst line or failed appliance is an emergency water removal job first, with extraction units pulling the bulk before drying. If framing stays damp past 48 hours, ask about mold remediation. No mold job starts until the moisture source is repaired, and health questions go to a physician.
