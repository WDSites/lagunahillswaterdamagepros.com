How coverage works

Crews are dispatched by address and by who is available that day. Same-day response is offered when a crew is available, and no arrival time is promised. Tell dispatch your street, the type of water and what is wet, and you will hear what comes next.

Each city sits within one or more water districts, and the district you call for a shut-off or a sewer question depends on your address. Check your water bill for the name. Districts in this area include El Toro Water District and Moulton Niguel Water District, with others serving parts of Mission Viejo and Lake Forest.

Who issues permits in each city

Permit rules vary by scope, and each city has its own office. Laguna Hills Community Development and Building and Safety are at the Civic Center, 24035 El Toro Road, phone (949) 707-2600. Mission Viejo runs Building Services under contract at 200 Civic Center and takes applications online. Laguna Woods lists its building permitting at 24264 El Toro Road, and its page notes that HOA approval does not replace a city permit.

For Lake Forest, Aliso Viejo and Laguna Niguel, check the city website or call city hall before starting structural, electrical or plumbing repair. Cosmetic repair is treated differently from structural work, so ask which category your job falls into.

A few facts that set each city apart

Mission Viejo

A contract city incorporated March 31, 1988, with hillside terrain that sends storm runoff downhill.

Lake Forest

Incorporated December 20, 1991. Foothill Ranch and Portola Hills joined in 2000, so older and newer neighborhoods sit side by side.

Aliso Viejo

Incorporated July 1, 2001. The city reports that 61 percent of its housing is multifamily or attached, so shared walls and ceilings matter.

Laguna Woods

Incorporated March 24, 1999. Laguna Woods Village, built from 1963 to 1985, is an age-restricted community with three mutuals.

Laguna Niguel

Incorporated December 1, 1989. More than a third of the city is open space, and the city's safety element maps FEMA flood zones in several places.

Laguna Hills

Incorporated December 20, 1991, after the Moulton Ranch was subdivided in the early 1960s. Home base for dispatch.
