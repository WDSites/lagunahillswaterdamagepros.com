Before you call

  • Shut off the main water valve if the source is a pipe and it is safe to reach.
  • Stay out of any water that may be sewage or touching electrical outlets.
  • Take photos of the damage before you move furniture.
  • Find your insurance policy number and the name of your water district from your bill.
  • Write down which rooms are wet and when it started.
