Why the first 48 hours decide the cleanup

Emergency water damage cleanup in Laguna Hills starts once the water source is stopped and the standing water is out. What remains is wet debris, damp contents and surfaces that need sanitizing. Left alone, those things turn into odor, staining and mold.

Call the 24/7 line as soon as you can. While you wait, wear gloves and boots, open doors to the wet room if the weather allows, and move photos, paperwork and electronics to a dry spot. Keep children and pets away from anything touched by gray or black water.

What cleanup actually covers

Debris and ruined materials

Soaked carpet pad, swollen particle board, wet insulation and crumbling drywall are bagged and hauled out so they stop holding water.

Surface cleaning

Hard surfaces are scrubbed with detergent first, then treated with a disinfectant used to its label directions, with dwell time respected.

Contents handling

Furniture, rugs, books and electronics are triaged. Hard goods are cleaned in place or moved out, and porous items are assessed one by one.

Odor control

Odor is tied to moisture and residue. Removing the source material and drying the area usually does more than any spray.

Gray and black water: what to do first

Not all water is equal. Gray water from a washer, dishwasher or overflowing sink with no waste carries bacteria and soap residue. Black water from a sewer, a toilet with solids or ground-surface flooding is heavily contaminated. Categories here follow ANSI/IICRC S500.

For gray or black water, stay out of the room. Do not run fans, do not wade in barefoot, and do not use a household vacuum. If water has reached an outlet, cut power at the panel only if the floor around it is dry, and call 911 or your utility for any electrical or gas concern before you call the restoration line.

How a cleanup visit runs

  1. Walk-through and photos

    The technician reviews each wet room, tests moisture and records the damage.

  2. Sort and remove

    Materials that cannot be saved are removed. Salvageable items are staged and cleaned.

  3. Clean and sanitize

    Surfaces are detergent-cleaned, then disinfected where the water category calls for it.

  4. Dry and deodorize

    Air movers and dehumidifiers finish the dry-out, and HEPA air scrubbers can run when odors or dust are an issue. See water mitigation for how drying is verified.

  5. Hand off to repair

    Once the rooms are clean and dry, the rebuild is scoped. Details are on the water damage repair page.

Hard water and the residue left behind

El Toro Water District's 2025 water quality report lists its imported supply at 270 mg/L of hardness, about 16 grains per gallon. When clean supply water floods a room and evaporates, minerals can stay behind as a white film on glass, tile and fixtures.

That is normal for water of this hardness, and it is one more reason cleanup includes wiping hard surfaces instead of simply drying them. Which water district serves your address depends on your street, so check your bill.
