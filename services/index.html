How the services fit together

Most jobs move in order: extraction, mitigation, cleanup, then repair. Flood and sewage jobs add containment early. Mold work comes in when growth already exists or when a past leak was never dried properly.

You do not have to book every stage with the same crew, but handing the job from one stage to the next is smoother when the records follow along. Moisture readings, photos and scope notes help your insurer and your next contractor.
